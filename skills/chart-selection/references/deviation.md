## Part 8 — Deviation

### 8.1 Diverging bar
- **Use when:** signed values vs. a reference — variance vs. budget, net sentiment. The zero/target line is the chart's most important line: solid, dark.

### 8.2 Diverging lollipop
- **Use when:** diverging bar with many categories — less ink, more rows.

### 8.3 Surplus/deficit filled line
- **Use when:** two series (or series vs. baseline) where the *gap* is the story — fill between them, one color per sign (trade balance, actual vs. plan).

### 8.4 Anomaly bars
- **Use when:** long series vs. baseline as +/- colored bars (temperature anomalies, error vs. forecast).

### 8.5 Warming stripes
- **Use when:** maximum compression of an anomaly series to pure color — one stripe per period. Communication, not analysis.

### 8.6 Line vs. reference band
- **Use when:** series against a target/normal range: gray band (target ± tolerance, historical p10–p90), actual line on top; excursions self-evident.
