## Part 12 — Finance & Quant

### 12.1 Candlestick / OHLC — see §3.9–3.10.

### 12.2 Equity curve
- **Use when:** cumulative strategy/portfolio value over time — log scale for long horizons (equal slopes = equal returns).

### 12.3 Drawdown / underwater chart
- **Use when:** the equity curve's honest twin: % below running peak, always ≤ 0. Pain, depth, and recovery time — pair it beneath the equity curve.

### 12.4 Monthly return heatmap
- **Use when:** year × month grid, diverging color on returns, annual totals as the right column — seasonality and bad regimes at a glance.

### 12.5 Rolling correlation / rolling beta
- **Use when:** relationship stability over time — a line of windowed estimates (with the window stated) instead of one misleading full-sample number.

### 12.6 Efficient frontier
- **Use when:** risk (σ) vs. return scatter of assets/portfolios + the frontier curve; tangency and current portfolio marked.

### 12.7 Yield curve / term structure
- **Use when:** rate vs. maturity, multiple dates overlaid (today vs. 1M/1Y ago) — inversions and shifts are the story. Small multiples or animation for evolution.

### 12.8 Order-book depth chart
- **Use when:** cumulative bid/ask volume around mid-price — two opposing step-areas; imbalance visible as asymmetry.

### 12.9 Volume profile
- **Use when:** traded volume by *price level* (horizontal histogram beside the price chart) — support/resistance by where volume lived.

### 12.10 Renko / Kagi / point-and-figure
- **Use when:** price movement filtered of time — bricks/lines drawn only on threshold moves. Technical-analysis idioms; know the audience.

### 12.11 Spread chart
- **Use when:** pairs/relative-value: the *difference or ratio* of two series plotted directly, with entry/exit bands — never two lines on dual axes.

### 12.12 Risk-contribution treemap
- **Use when:** portfolio decomposition where size = weight or risk contribution and color = return — the trading-floor heatmap.
