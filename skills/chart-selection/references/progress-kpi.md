## Part 13 — Progress & KPI

### 13.1 Bullet chart
- **Use when:** KPI vs. target vs. qualitative bands — the dense, honest gauge replacement.

### 13.2 KPI tile
- **Use when:** headline metrics: big number + delta vs. reference + sparkline. Number first, chart second.

### 13.3 Progress donut / ring
- **Use when:** single completion % where the circular form is idiomatic (goals, storage). Fine at exactly one value.

### 13.4 Waffle progress
- **Use when:** completion as filled squares — countable, honest.

### 13.5 Thermometer
- **Use when:** fundraising-style single target progress; schematic by convention.

### 13.6 Gauge / speedometer
- Listed for completeness: huge pixels, one number, no context. Use a bullet. (See anti-patterns.)
