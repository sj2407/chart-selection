## Part 10 — Uncertainty

### 10.1 Error bars
- **Use when:** point estimates + interval per category. Always say what the bar is (SE? 95% CI? SD?) — unlabeled error bars are noise.

### 10.2 Confidence band
- **Use when:** interval around a fitted line/series.

### 10.3 Fan chart — see §3.20.

### 10.4 Gradient / fade plot
- **Use when:** encoding uncertainty as fading opacity — no hard interval edge, so no false cliff at "95%."

### 10.5 Quantile dot plot
- **Use when:** communicating outcome probabilities to lay audiences as countable dots ("19 of 20 dots are above zero"). Best-evidenced format for probabilistic decisions.

### 10.6 Hypothetical outcome plots (HOPs)
- **Use when:** animated draws from the distribution — people internalize variability from frames better than from bands. Interactive contexts.

### 10.7 Eye / half-eye plot
- **Use when:** posterior distributions per parameter: density + interval + point, one glyph (Bayesian reporting).
